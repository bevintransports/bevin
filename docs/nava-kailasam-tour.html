Nava Kailasam Tour Taxi — FAQs

Do you provide taxi service for the Nava Kailasam temple tour?

Yes. Bevin Transports runs full-day taxi service covering all nine Nava Kailasam Shiva temples along the Tirunelveli-Tuticorin belt, from Tiruchendur or Thoothukudi, ideal for pilgrims wanting to complete the circuit in a single day.

How long does the Nava Kailasam tour take?

A full circuit typically takes a full day. Starting early in the morning is recommended to comfortably cover all nine temples with time for darshan at each stop.

What vehicle is best for the Nava Kailasam tour?

Sedan or SUV works well for individuals and families. For larger pilgrim groups, a Tempo Traveller is recommended for comfort across the full-day circuit.

How much does the Nava Kailasam tour taxi cost?

Call or WhatsApp +91 63804 58832 for the current package fare. Bevin Transports quotes a fixed price before you book, with driver bata and toll disclosed upfront.

Can I book the Nava Kailasam tour from Thoothukudi as well as Tiruchendur?

Yes. We offer pickup from either Tiruchendur or Thoothukudi for the full Nava Kailasam circuit.

Is the trip GPS-tracked for safety?

Yes. Trips can be GPS-tracked with live location sharing available on request, so family members can follow the journey in real time.
