Taxi & cab service FAQs

Is there a taxi service near Tiruchendur Murugan Temple?

Yes. Bevin Transports runs 24/7 call taxi and pilgrimage cabs right up to Tiruchendur Murugan Temple (Arulmigu Subramanya Swamy Temple), including waiting-and-return trips and group pilgrimage vehicles.

How do I book a taxi from Thoothukudi Airport to Tiruchendur?

Call or WhatsApp us at +91 63804 58832 with your flight details. We track arrivals, meet you at Thoothukudi Airport (TCR) and drop you in Tiruchendur — around 38 km, 45–50 minutes — with early-morning 4 AM pickups available.

Do you provide taxi service from Thoothukudi Railway Station to Tiruchendur?

Yes. We pick up right from the station exit and drop directly at Tiruchendur town, temple or beach, any time of day or night.

Can I book a cab from Tirunelveli Junction to Tiruchendur?

Yes. We offer one-way drop and round-trip taxi from Tirunelveli Junction Railway Station to Tiruchendur, around 65 km, in Sedan, SUV or Innova.

Is taxi service available to Manapad and Kulasekarapattinam beaches?

Yes. We run local and outstation cabs to Manapad Beach and Holy Cross Church, and to Kulasekarapattinam Beach and Mutharamman Temple, from Tiruchendur, Thoothukudi and Tirunelveli.

What is the taxi fare from Tiruchendur to Kulasekarapattinam?

Kulasekarapattinam is about 20 km from Tiruchendur. Call +91 63804 58832 for the current fare — transparent per-km pricing, no hidden charges.

What is the call taxi number in Tiruchendur?

Call Bevin Transports at +91 63804 58832, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for immediate dispatch.

Is there a taxi service from Tiruchendur to Rameswaram?

Yes. Bevin Transports offers one-way drop and round-trip outstation taxi from Tiruchendur to Rameswaram — around 223 km, approximately 4.5 hours — in Sedan, SUV, Innova or Tempo Traveller.

Do you provide taxi service for the Nava Thirupathi temple tour?

Yes. We run full-day taxi service covering all nine Nava Thirupathi temples along the Tiruchendur–Tirunelveli road, ideal for pilgrims wanting to complete the circuit in a single day. Early morning starts recommended.

Is there a call taxi to Uvari from Tiruchendur?

Yes. We offer local and outstation cabs to Uvari, around 40 km from Tiruchendur, for visits to St. Antony's Shrine, a well-known pilgrimage site on the coast.

What is the taxi fare from Tiruchendur to Kanyakumari?

Kanyakumari is approximately 80 km from Tiruchendur via Nagercoil. Call +91 63804 58832 for the current fare — transparent per-km pricing with driver bata and toll disclosed before booking.

Do you offer taxi service from Madurai Airport to Tiruchendur?

Yes. We provide flight-tracked pickup from Madurai Airport (IXM) to Tiruchendur and drop from Tiruchendur to Madurai Airport, approximately 150 km each way, with early morning and late-night availability.

Is there a taxi from Tiruchendur to Madurai?

Yes. Bevin Transports offers one-way drop and round-trip outstation taxi from Tiruchendur to Madurai, around 181 km, approximately 3.5–4 hours, in Sedan, SUV, Innova or Tempo Traveller.

Do you provide taxi service from Tiruchendur to Trivandrum Airport?

Yes. We offer flight-tracked outstation taxi from Tiruchendur to Trivandrum Airport (TRV), approximately 180 km, around 4.5 hours, in Sedan, SUV or Tempo Traveller.

Is there a taxi from Tiruchendur to Trichy?
