Tiruchendur Murugan Temple

Arulmigu Subramanya Swamy Temple, one of the six sacred abodes (Arupadaiveedu) of Lord Murugan, right on the coast. 24/7 call taxi, waiting-and-return trips, and group pilgrimage vehicles for festival days.

Locally known as Kulasai Mutharamman Kovil, famed for its Aadi festival crowds and coastal setting. We run local and outstation taxi service here from Tiruchendur, Thoothukudi, and Tirunelveli, including festival-season trips.

Christian Pilgrimage Sites

Churches near Tiruchendur

The Thoothukudi coast has a deep Portuguese-era Catholic heritage, with several major shrines within easy driving distance.

~35 km, Manapad

Shrine of the Holy Cross, Manapad

A significant Christian pilgrimage site on the coast, often combined with a Manapad Beach visit.

~40 km, Thoothukudi

Basilica of Our Lady of Snows (OLS Church)

A minor basilica on Beach Road, Thoothukudi, built by the Portuguese in 1711 — one of the region's most visited Catholic shrines.

~35 km, Uvari

Church of St. Antony, Uvari

A well-known coastal parish church, often visited alongside the Uvari Suyambulingaswamy Temple nearby.

~45 km, Sokankudiyiruppu

Athisaya Manal Matha Shrine

Known locally as Manal Matha Kovil ("Our Lady of Red Sands"), surrounded by red sand dunes and palm trees near Uvari.

Temples Near Tiruchendur — FAQs

What are the most famous temples near Tiruchendur?

The most visited are the Tiruchendur Murugan Temple, Kulasekarapattinam Mutharamman Kovil, the Nava Thirupathi circuit of nine Perumal (Vishnu) temples, and the Nava Kailasam circuit of nine Shiva temples — all within a short drive.

Is Nava Thirupathi the same as Nava Kailasam?

No. Nava Thirupathi is a circuit of nine Perumal (Vishnu) temples along the Thamirabarani river. Nava Kailasam is a separate circuit of nine Shiva temples, also along the Thamirabarani river. We run dedicated taxi tours for both.

Do you provide taxi service to Kulasekarapattinam Mutharamman Kovil?

Yes. We run local and outstation taxi service to Kulasekarapattinam Mutharamman Kovil from Tiruchendur, Thoothukudi, and Tirunelveli, including festival-season trips during the annual Aadi festival.

Do you provide taxi service to churches near Tiruchendur?

Yes. We offer taxi service to the major Catholic pilgrimage sites near Tiruchendur, including the Shrine of the Holy Cross in Manapad, the Basilica of Our Lady of Snows in Thoothukudi, the Church of St. Antony in Uvari, and the Athisaya Manal Matha Shrine near Uvari.

Can I visit both temples and churches in the same trip?

Yes. Many pilgrims combine a temple visit with a nearby church in the same trip — for example Uvari Suyambulingaswamy Temple with the Church of St. Antony, Uvari, or Manapad's Shrine of the Holy Cross with the Tiruchendur Murugan Temple. We help plan the full itinerary.

Can I combine multiple temples in one taxi trip?

Yes. We plan combined pilgrimage itineraries covering the Tiruchendur Murugan Temple, Kulasekarapattinam Mutharamman Kovil, and either the Nava Thirupathi or Nava Kailasam circuit, depending on how much time you have.

What vehicle is best for a multi-temple pilgrimage trip?

Sedan or SUV works well for individuals and families. For larger pilgrim groups visiting multiple temples in one day, a Tempo Traveller is recommended for comfort.
