The Major Shrines

Five churches, one coast

~35 km, Manapad

Shrine of the Holy Cross, Manapad

A pilgrimage chapel on the Manapad headland, one of the coast's most visited Christian sites, alongside the parish church of St. Thomas.

~40 km, Thoothukudi

Basilica of Our Lady of Snows (OLS Church)

A minor basilica on Beach Road, Thoothukudi, first built in the 18th century by the Portuguese — one of the most visited Catholic shrines on this coast.

~35 km, Uvari

Church of St. Antony, Uvari

A well-known coastal parish church, often visited alongside the Uvari Suyambulingaswamy Temple nearby.

~45 km, Sokankudiyiruppu

Athisaya Manal Matha Shrine

Known locally as Manal Matha Kovil ("Our Lady of Red Sands"), surrounded by striking red sand dunes near Uvari.

~40 km, Thoothukudi

Sacred Heart Cathedral

The mother church of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Tuticorin, seat of the Bishop of Thoothukudi.

Churches in Thoothukudi District — FAQs

What are the most famous churches in Thoothukudi district?

The most visited are the Shrine of the Holy Cross in Manapad, the Basilica of Our Lady of Snows in Thoothukudi (the "OLS Church"), the Church of St. Antony in Uvari, the Athisaya Manal Matha Shrine near Uvari, and Sacred Heart Cathedral in Thoothukudi, the mother church of the Diocese of Tuticorin.

Do you provide taxi service to Manapad Church?

Yes. We run taxi service to the Shrine of the Holy Cross in Manapad, approximately 35 km from Tiruchendur, often combined with a Manapad Beach visit.

What is the Thoothukudi OLS Church?

OLS Church refers to the Basilica of Our Lady of Snows in Thoothukudi, a minor basilica built by the Portuguese and one of the most visited Catholic shrines on the Tamil Nadu coast.

Do you provide taxi service to Manal Matha Kovil?

Yes. We run taxi service to the Athisaya Manal Matha Shrine, known locally as Manal Matha Kovil, near Uvari, surrounded by red sand dunes.

Can I visit multiple churches in one taxi trip?

Yes. Many pilgrims combine Manapad, Uvari, and the Manal Matha Shrine in a single day trip, since all three are along the same coastal road south of Tiruchendur.
