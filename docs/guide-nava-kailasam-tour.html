Nava Kailasam Tour: What to Expect in a Day

Nine Shiva temples, one river, a different circuit entirely from Nava Thirupathi. Here's what the day actually looks like.

Nava Kailasam is a circuit of nine temples dedicated to Shiva, worshipped as Kailasanathar at each site, spread along the Thamirabarani river across Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi districts. Each temple in the circuit is traditionally associated with one of the nine Navagraha (planetary) deities, which is part of what makes the circuit distinct from other regional temple routes.

How the Day Usually Runs

Like most multi-temple circuits in this region, an early start matters. Most temples observe a midday closing period, so pilgrims aiming to cover all nine in a day typically begin by 6:30–7 AM. Expect the full circuit to take 8 to 10 hours including driving time, darshan, and a lunch break — this is a full-day commitment, not a half-day add-on.

The nine temples aren't in a single town — they're spread across a stretch of villages along both banks of the river, which means route planning matters. A driver who already knows the sequence saves real time compared to navigating between unfamiliar village temples one by one.

What Makes It Distinct

Where Nava Thirupathi has a strong literary connection through the Alvars' hymns, Nava Kailasam's character comes more from its Navagraha associations — each temple is tied to a specific planet, and many pilgrims visit specifically to address a particular graha dosha (planetary affliction) in their horoscope, in addition to general worship.

The temples themselves tend to be quieter and less crowded than the more famous single-destination temples like Tiruchendur's own Murugan Temple, giving the circuit a more contemplative, unhurried feel.

Practical Tips

  • Start early to make the most of the pre-midday darshan window at each temple.
  • Carry water and snacks — smaller village temples don't always have food stalls nearby.
  • Dress modestly, as is standard at any Tamil Nadu temple.
  • If you're doing this specifically for Navagraha worship, mention it to your driver — the visiting order can sometimes be adjusted to prioritize a specific planetary temple.
  • Consider pairing this with the Nava Thirupathi circuit on a separate day if you have the time, since both routes run through similar territory but visit entirely different temples.

How This Differs from Nava Thirupathi

This is the most common point of confusion, so it's worth restating clearly: Nava Kailasam is nine Shiva temples. Nava Thirupathi is a separate circuit of nine Vishnu (Perumal) temples. They share the same general river geography but are entirely different sets of temples, and most pilgrims treat them as two separate day trips rather than combining them.

Nava Kailasam Tour — FAQs

How many temples are in the Nava Kailasam circuit?

Nine temples, each dedicated to Shiva as Kailasanathar, located along the Thamirabarani river in Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi districts.

How long does the Nava Kailasam tour take?

A full circuit of all nine temples typically takes a full day, around 8 to 10 hours including travel time and darshan at each temple.

Is Nava Kailasam the same as Nava Thirupathi?

No. Nava Kailasam is a circuit of nine Shiva temples. Nava Thirupathi is a separate circuit of nine Vishnu (Perumal) temples. Both run along the Thamirabarani river but visit different temples.
